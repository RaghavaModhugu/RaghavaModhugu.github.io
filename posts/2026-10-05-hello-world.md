I've written a lot of research papers and design docs. They follow strict rules: state the problem, show the numbers, cite your sources. That discipline is useful, but it leaves no room for the half-formed ideas that come up between experiments.

This space is for those ideas.

## What to expect

- **Articles** on computer vision, 3D perception, and what it takes to get ML from a paper into production.
- **Notes** on building and leading engineering teams.
- **Short stories**, because not everything has to be about work.

> Writing is thinking on paper. If I can't explain it simply, I probably don't understand it yet.
