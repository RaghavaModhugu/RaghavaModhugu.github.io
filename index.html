Hi, I'm Raghava.

I'm curious about how machines come to see and understand the world, and about how we understand it ourselves. Most days I work out how a camera can make sense of the space around it. Outside work I'm drawn to philosophical enquiry, especially logic and Buddhist thought, and I read and listen to a lot of Indian history. Sometimes I write stories.

What I'm exploring

Seeing in 3D from a single camera

How much of the world's shape and scale can you recover from ordinary video? I keep coming back to structure-from-motion, localisation and mapping.

The world through someone else's eyes

Working on Ego4D made me curious about first-person video: what it captures about attention, habits and how we use our hands.

Small devices, big models

I like the craft of making large models run fast on small hardware, and the trade-offs that forces you to think about.

Logic & Buddhist thought

How do we know what we know? I'm drawn to questions of reasoning and perception, from formal logic to the Buddhist traditions that thought hard about mind and inference.

Indian history

I read and listen to a lot of Indian history: its empires, ideas, and the people and traditions that shaped how the subcontinent thinks.

Stories

Sometimes ideas from all of the above turn into essays or short fiction.
